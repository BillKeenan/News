What to Expect

Each September, the brothers of N.E.W.S. saddle up for a celebration of autumn in northern Ontario. This year, we're heading to Manitoulin Island — a spectacular riding destination with breathtaking fall colours, welcoming roads, and endless fellowship.

What's included: Lots of fun, food & fellowship. Riding adventures, camaraderie, and memories that last all year.
