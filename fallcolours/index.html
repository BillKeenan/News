Join Us for a Spectacular Ride!

Experience the breathtaking fall colours of Manitoulin Island on a guided motorcycle ride. This is a relaxed, fun gathering for riders of all skill levels to celebrate the season and brotherhood on two wheels.

📅 Meet at Hutchinson's Cottages and Campground by 5:00 PM Friday

What's Included

  • Accommodations at Hutchinson's Cottages and Campground
  • Meals: Friday chilli dinner, Saturday breakfast, Saturday dinner
  • Saturday lunch: Self-purchase at a restaurant (on your own)
  • Guided day tour around Manitoulin Island through fall scenery
  • Fellowship with fellow riders from across the district
